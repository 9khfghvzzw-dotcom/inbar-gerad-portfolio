# Behavioural data & statistical modelling

Research work connecting psychological questions with quantitative analysis in R.

## Methods

The workflow covers latent profile analysis, model comparison, regression, graphical summaries, and automated research tables. It connects analysis decisions with clear reporting.

## Research context

As a research assistant in the Cognitive Neuroscience Lab at Ben-Gurion University, I collect, clean, and analyse behavioural data using R and Python.

This is a methods overview. Participant data and unpublished findings are not included. Contact me to discuss my contribution and the analysis workflow.
